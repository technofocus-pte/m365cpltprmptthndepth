### Sending tips
- If your test account can't easily impersonate multiple senders, you can send all 15 from one or two accounts and just vary the **Subject** line and body — the lab exercise cares about grouping/triage logic, not authentic sender domains.
- Keep the **subjects exactly as written** so the "Re:" threading (emails #1 → #9 → #12, and #3 → #15) shows up correctly in your inbox for the triage exercise.
- Backdate or schedule sends across Mon–Thu if your mail client supports it, so the "this week" time window in the strong prompt has something real to filter on.
